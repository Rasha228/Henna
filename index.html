Behind the cone… it's me

Hi, I'm Ayushi · the artist behind Henna By Ayushi. What began as a love for the art of mehendi has grown into a passion for creating meaningful bridal designs that become part of a bride's most cherished memories. I specialise in elegant, detailed bridal mehendi, thoughtfully designed around each bride's personal style, story and vision. It is not only beautiful henna · it is an opportunity to make you feel confident, celebrated and truly yourself.
