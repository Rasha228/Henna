Shipping, pickup & returns

Fresh henna products are made to order. This page explains how Canadian shipping, GTA pickup and product concerns are handled.

Shipping within Canada

Henna By Ayushi currently ships within Canada. Orders are generally prepared within 1–3 business days after payment is received. Henna is handmade in small batches, so preparation may take longer during holidays, sales or high-volume periods. If there is a significant delay, we will contact you.

Shipping rates and courier delivery estimates are confirmed according to your location and selected service. Delivery estimates are provided by the carrier and are not guaranteed.

Tracking, delays & lost packages

When tracking is available, it is shared after the order has been shipped. Once an order has been handed to the carrier, delivery is managed by that carrier. Delays may be affected by weather, holidays, incorrect addresses or other circumstances outside our control.

If a parcel is delayed, lost or marked delivered but cannot be found, please contact us with your order number. We will do our best to assist with available tracking information and a carrier investigation. Please first check household members, neighbours and the delivery location if tracking shows delivery.

Henna By Ayushi is not responsible for packages lost after being marked as delivered, or for delays caused by circumstances outside its control.

Free GTA pickup

Pickup is free in the GTA. Please place pickup orders at least one day in advance. Pickup is available daily from 5 PM to 9 PM. The pickup address is supplied only after your order has been confirmed. Please call 639-571-9695 before pickup.

Incorrect addresses & damaged orders

Customers are responsible for entering a complete and accurate shipping address. If an order is returned because an address is incorrect or incomplete, additional shipping charges may apply to resend it.

Because henna is perishable, returns and exchanges are not accepted unless a product arrives damaged or incorrect. If your package arrives damaged, please take clear photographs of the packaging and contents and contact us within 24 hours of delivery. We will review the issue and may offer a replacement, refund, store credit or another appropriate resolution.

Product care

Freeze cones as soon as they arrive. They can be stored in the freezer for 3–4 months and thawed at room temperature as needed. Stain results vary by skin type, body temperature, placement and aftercare. Patch testing is recommended, particularly for sensitive skin or essential-oil sensitivities.
